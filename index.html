About Ultimate Pro

About the Application

Ultimate Pro is a professional employee management system designed to help individual workers, freelancers, and employees track their working hours, manage salary calculations, and understand their financial and legal rights.

Our mission is to empower employees with the tools and knowledge they need to manage their careers effectively, ensure fair compensation, and make informed financial decisions.

Key Features

Shift Tracking

Record your work hours with detailed timestamps and break tracking

Salary Calculations

Automatic calculation of gross, net, taxes, and deductions

Analytics

Visualize your earning trends and work patterns

Pension Planning

Project your retirement savings and pension income

Benefits & Insurance

Understand your insurance coverage and benefits

Reports

Generate detailed reports for taxes and verification

Rights & Responsibilities

As an employee in Israel, you have significant legal protections under Israeli labor law. This application helps you understand your rights, but professional consultation is essential for specific situations.

Key areas covered by Israeli law include working hours, minimum wage, overtime, vacation days, severance pay, pension contributions, and protection from discrimination and harassment.
